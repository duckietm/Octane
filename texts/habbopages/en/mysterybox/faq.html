Mystery Box

A Mystery Box holds a surprise prize, but you can never open it alone. Every box is locked, and the key that fits it is always in the hands of another player. Find them, open the box together, and you both win.

What do I get?

You receive two things: a box and a key. Each has a colour, and they are never the same colour. Your key does not fit your own box. It fits somebody else's.

There are eight colours: blue, green, lilac, orange, purple, red, turquoise and yellow.

Which colours do I have?

Look at the Mystery Box panel on the right side of your screen. It shows the colour of your box and the colour of your key. Click its title to fold it away.

How do I open a box?

  1. Find a player whose key has the same colour as your box.
  2. Meet in a room. Place your box, and let the other player place their key.
  3. Click your box and choose Open box. A window tells you both that the box is waiting.
  4. The other player clicks their key and chooses Use key.
  5. The box opens. You both get a prize!

What do we win?

Each of you gets your own surprise prize, straight into your inventory. The box and the key are used up: they disappear once the box has been opened.

Can I help someone else?

Yes, and you should! Your key opens the box of a player whose box has your key's colour. When they start opening their box, click your key and use it. You win a prize too.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. While the box is waiting, either of you can cancel. The owner chooses Cancel opening, the key holder chooses Remove your key. Nothing is lost: the box and the key stay yours and you can try again later.

If nobody answers, the waiting simply stops after a while.

Nothing happens when I click my box

  • There is no key of the right colour in the room. The key has to be placed in the same room as the box.
  • The key in the room is your own. A box only opens with a key that belongs to another player.
  • The colours do not match. Check the panel on the right side of your screen.

Tips

  • Ask around in busy rooms: "Who has a red key?" works wonders.
  • Trade favours. Open someone's box with your key, and they may help you find yours.
  • Never give away your account details to get a box opened. Nobody needs them.
