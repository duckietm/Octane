Writing forum messages

A good post is easy to read. Here is what you can do in a Group Forum message, and a few tips to make yours clear.

Subject and message

A new thread needs a subject and a message. Pick a subject that says what the thread is about, so others can find it later. A reply only needs a message.

Paragraphs

Press Enter to start a new line. Leave an empty line between two thoughts: short paragraphs read much more easily than one long block.

Quoting someone

To answer a specific message, click the reply button on that message. Its text is put into your reply with > in front of every line, under a line that says who wrote it and when. Write your own answer below the quote.

· You may shorten a quote to the part you are answering.

· A quote inside a quote is left out, so replies do not grow endlessly.


What does not work

Messages are plain text. Colours, fonts, pictures and HTML are not shown, so do not try to add them.

Tips

· Read the thread before you reply: your question may already be answered.

· Stay on the subject. Start a new thread for a new subject.

· No shouting in capitals, and no spam.

· The hotel rules apply here as well. Messages that break them can be hidden by the group's moderators or by hotel staff.
