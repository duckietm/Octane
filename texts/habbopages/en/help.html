Help

Stuck, curious, or is someone spoiling your fun? This page tells you where to look and who to ask.

I need help right now

Click Help at the top right of your screen. From there you can ask a question, report a player or a room, and see the result of earlier reports.

Someone is bothering me

· Ignore them: click the player and choose Ignore. You will no longer see what they say.

· Report them: click the player and choose Report, or use the Help button. Pick the messages that show what happened, so a moderator can see it for themselves.

· Leave: you can always walk out of a room. Nobody can keep you there.


Keep your account safe

· Never tell anyone your password. Staff will never ask for it.

· Nobody needs your account details to give you credits, furni or a prize. Anyone who says so is trying to trick you.

· Do not share personal details such as your full name, address, phone number or school.

· Use a password you do not use anywhere else, and change it if you think someone knows it.


Trading safely

Only trade through the trade window, where both of you can see what is offered before you accept. Check the items again right before you confirm. A deal that sounds too good to be true usually is.

I have been muted or banned

A sanction is given when the hotel rules are broken. Open Help to see your current sanction and how long it lasts. If you think it is a mistake, explain what happened calmly through the Help tool.

Where do I find things?

· Rooms: open the Navigator from the toolbar and search by room name, owner, group or tag.

· Furni: open the Catalogue. What you buy goes to your Inventory.

· Friends: use the friend bar at the bottom of the screen to chat, follow a friend or find new ones.

· Chat commands: click the small help button beside the chat bar for the full list.


Be a good neighbour

Treat others the way you want to be treated. No bullying, no scamming, no hateful or sexual talk. The hotel is for everyone, and a friendly word goes a long way.
