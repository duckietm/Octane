What is HC Payday?

HC Payday

On the 15th of every month, HC members get 10% of their Catalogue purchases back. These credits are deposited in your earnings window in the HC Payday section. They also get another payment on the same
day, which is determined by their HC Streak. HC Streaks are the length of time a
Habbo has maintained an active HC membership. Below are the different
thresholds and the related bonuses:

· 7 days = 5 credits

· 30 days = 10 credits

· 60 days = 15 credits

· 90 days = 20 credits

· 180 days = 25 credits

· 365 days = 30 credits
