Special chat commands

(Hold shift while you press enter) Shout and your text appears in bold

  • :fluister <username> - Whisper to another Camwijs
  • :mutepets - Ignore pet chat
  • :mutebots - Ignore Bot chat
  • o/ or _o/ - Wave
  • :idle - Sleep
  • _b - Thumbs up
  • :sign <a number from 0-10> - Show a number sign
  • :sign <a number from 11-17> - Show a special sign
  • :pickall - Pick up all your furni from the room
  • :ejectall - Remove all your furni from a group room
  • :sit* - Sit down
  • :stand* - Stand up

Builders Club Commands

  • :pickallbc - Pick up all your Builders Club furni from the room

For Camwijs Club Members Only

  • :kiss - Blow a kiss
  • :D or ;D - Laugh
  • :chooser - See who is in your room
  • :furni - See which furni you have
  • :kick <username> - Kick a Camwijs out of the room if you are a room manager
  • :kickall - Kick everyone out of the room if you are a room manager
  • :floor* - Edit the floor
  • :bcfloor* - Edit the floor
