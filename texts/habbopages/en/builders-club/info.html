Builders Club

Builders Club is a membership for players who love to build. Instead of buying every furni, you borrow it from the Builders Club Warehouse and place it straight into your room.

What do I get?

· Access to the Builders Club Warehouse, a catalogue full of furni you can place without paying for each piece.

· A furni limit: the number of Warehouse furni you may have placed at the same time, across all your rooms.

· As many rebuilds as you like. Pick a furni up and its place in your limit is free again.


How do I build with it?

· Open the Builders Club catalogue from the toolbar.

· Choose a furni and place it in your room. It does not go to your Inventory.

· Want another one of the same? Select the furni in your room and click Place more in its info window.


Where can I place Warehouse furni?

In rooms you own, and in group rooms where you are an administrator of the group. While you are on a free trial, a room has to be empty of visitors when you place furni.

What happens when my membership ends?

The rooms you built with Warehouse furni are locked for visitors until you renew. Nothing is thrown away: renew your membership and they open again exactly as you left them.

Can I trade or sell Warehouse furni?

No. Warehouse furni is borrowed, not owned. It cannot be traded, sold, gifted or recycled, and it never appears in your Inventory.
